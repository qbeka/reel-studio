Reel

A product demo you can actually use

Details

Reel stores the commands of a workflow, so playback runs the real app and taking over hands you a working copy of that moment, which never reaches the recording.

The receipts here are synthetic, with no real people, receipts or balances behind them. The editor records scenes written for Reel and exports a static viewer that needs no backend.

Results

Seeking a long log
17 ms at p95to any point of a generated 20,000 command log, 16.7 to 17.6 across 5 runs, against 973 ms replaying from the start
Seeking correctness
0 mismatchesagainst replay at 5,332 positions
Seeded failures
14 of 14 detectedoverall, including 6 of 6 seeded reducer bugs that the replay comparison could not see, caught by a separate BigInt model of the money

Limits

  • The gain only appears on a generated stress log, because on the real 10 command recording here both variants take 1.1 ms.
  • The first seek in a fresh page is slower, and on the stress log the slowest of four takes about 1.1 s.
  • Timings come from one Apple M5 Pro laptop in headless Chromium, with other work running.
  • It records only scenes written for it, so it cannot record any website.
  • No study has been run, so nobody outside this project has been measured using it.
